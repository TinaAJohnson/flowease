# FLOWEASE Community Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

FLOWEASE is committed to providing a welcoming, harassment-free, and inclusive community for all members, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity, sexual orientation, or socioeconomic status.

We are dedicated to fostering a respectful, collaborative, and supportive environment where every member feels safe to contribute ideas, ask questions, and grow.

## Our Standards

### Expected Behavior

We expect all community members to:

- **Be respectful** — Treat all community members with dignity and courtesy
- **Be inclusive** — Welcome and support people from diverse backgrounds and perspectives
- **Communicate constructively** — Engage in thoughtful, honest, and professional dialogue
- **Give credit** — Acknowledge contributions and credit others' work appropriately
- **Be patient and understanding** — Remember that people may have different communication styles and experiences
- **Report issues** — Notify maintainers of violations or concerns promptly and privately
- **Assume good intent** — Approach disagreements charitably and seek to understand different viewpoints

### Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not tolerated:

- Harassment, bullying, or intimidation of any kind
- Discrimination based on protected characteristics (race, gender, religion, sexual orientation, disability, etc.)
- Hate speech, slurs, or dehumanizing language
- Sexual harassment or inappropriate advances
- Threats, violence, or violent language
- Doxing, doxxing, or sharing private information without consent
- Spam, trolling, or deliberate disruption of community spaces
- Repeated, unwelcome contact after being asked to stop
- Any form of abuse or exploitation

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- All FLOWEASE project spaces (GitHub, discussions, pull requests, issues, wiki)
- Public and private communications related to the project
- Conferences, meetups, and events representing FLOWEASE
- Third-party spaces where FLOWEASE community members gather (Slack, Discord, etc.)

It applies to everyone who participates in the FLOWEASE community, including maintainers, contributors, sponsors, and users.

## Enforcement

### Reporting

If you witness or experience a violation of this Code of Conduct, please report it by:

1. **Email:** Email the project maintainers at [contact email to be added]
2. **Github Issues:** For less urgent matters, you may open a private security advisory
3. **Direct Communication:** Feel free to reach out to any trusted maintainer

**All reports will be:**
- Treated confidentially
- Investigated promptly and fairly
- Handled with sensitivity to the reporter's safety

### Consequences

Violations of the Code of Conduct may result in:

1. **Warning** — A private message explaining the violation and expectations
2. **Temporary Restriction** — Temporary suspension from community spaces (24 hours to 30 days)
3. **Permanent Ban** — Removal from the project and all related spaces
4. **Escalation** — Involvement of law enforcement if necessary (for threats or harassment)

Consequences are proportional to the severity and pattern of the violation. Repeated violations will lead to escalated actions.

## Appeal Process

If you believe a moderation decision was made in error, you may appeal by:

1. Sending a detailed explanation to the project maintainers
2. Including any relevant context or evidence
3. Allowing 7 days for a response

Appeals will be reviewed by the same team that made the initial decision (or an alternative maintainer if there's a conflict of interest).
